# Election geography resolver

The Python resolver validates identities and emits auditable joins. The Swift resolver checks the elected year’s date contract and interprets the selected renderer’s feature key. A result and its declared geographic vintage move together when the election changes. When two years share the same source, the lab changes paint without rebuilding the source or Albers projection.

| Mode | Selection rule |
| --- | --- |
| Electoral College, 1788–2000 | Use the original lab’s inclusive civil-day state and territory snapshot. 1788 uses February 4, 1789. The 1792–1844 dates use the scheduled Electoral College vote. Starting in 1848, the contract uses federal Election Day. State and D.C. record IDs carry eligible allocations. Territories and nonparticipating jurisdictions receive neutral fills. |
| Electoral College, 2004–2024 | Use the approved November 7, 2000 state snapshot and disclose both election day and boundary date. At-large and district allocation records join to the parent state and produce stripes where candidates differ. |
| Standard county results | Match string Census GEOID, state and corresponding jurisdiction name. The 2024 Census source is explicitly a fallback in 2000–2020. Withhold known incompatible matches and preserve their returns. Handle Virginia independent cities as their own county equivalents whenever their identifiers and boundaries correspond. |
| Connecticut | Remove the Census planning-region features from the county-result display source. Match the eight supplied traditional county polygons using the explicit county-name/FIPS crosswalk. The archival vintage of the supplied feature file remains unverified. |
| Alaska | Use the 40 supplied 2024 State House districts. For 2000–2020 the label discloses a 2024 district fallback. A shared district number is evidence of a reporting label and does not validate polygon equivalence over time. District 99 stays outside the map. |

## Exceptions and vintage evidence

- Colorado created Broomfield in 2001 from portions of Adams, Boulder, Jefferson and Weld. Their 2000 results are withheld from the 2024 polygons. The 2000 Alleghany County and 2000–2012 Bedford County results are also withheld across independent-city changes. Shannon County changed its name and code from `46113` to Oglala Lakota `46102` in 2015. The documented rename permits this crosswalk, including the stale code on the 2024 row. [Census changes, 2000s](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/technical-documentation/county-changes.2000.html) · [Census changes, 2010s](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/technical-documentation/county-changes.2010.html).
- Connecticut’s traditional county FIPS are Fairfield `09001`, Hartford `09003`, Litchfield `09005`, Middlesex `09007`, New Haven `09009`, New London `09011`, Tolland `09013` and Windham `09015`. Statewide write-ins use `00000` and remain separate. [Census change to planning regions](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/06/06/2022-12063/change-to-county-equivalents-in-the-state-of-connecticut).
- Alaska’s 2000 geography predates the post-2000 amended plan. The 2004 and 2008 elections follow the 2002-era plan, 2012 used the plan in effect for that year’s general election, and the 2013 proclamation is the starting point for 2016 and 2020. Alaska publishes archived PDF maps and current district records. Matching historical vector geometries and source reporting coverage still need verification. The supplied 2024 asset records `LSY=2024` on every district. [2002 redistricting](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/doc/oep/2002/2002_oep_reg_3.pdf) · [2012 election plan](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/doc/bmp/2012/2012_prim_bmp_english.pdf) · [2013 archive](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/Core/Archive/2013districtmaps.php) · [2024 district maps](https://www.elections.alaska.gov/research/district-maps/).
- Kansas City appears as a separate Missouri election reporting jurisdiction. Its earlier place-like code and changed 2024 code do not authorize assigning the entire return to a county polygon. Maine UOCAVA, Rhode Island federal precinct and Connecticut statewide write-ins are retained as reporting records without polygons. D.C. is matched explicitly to county-equivalent `11001`. The report also inventories polygons without a source row, including Kalawao County in 2024. [Join coverage](ELECTION_JOIN_COVERAGE.md).

The local geometry retains source keys and vintage in the manifest. No county-derived state popular-vote totals are shown. Mixed reporting coverage, omitted rows and unreconciled counts prohibit treating the full collection of county rows as certified state totals.
