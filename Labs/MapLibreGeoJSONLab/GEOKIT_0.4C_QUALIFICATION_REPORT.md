# BalloticsGeoKit 0.4C qualification

**Decision B — qualified with documented restrictions.** The two Apple consumers now present one projected revision as a national editorial map, exploratory map, focused state or local inspection. The research qualification covers the tested geography, appearances and platform behavior.

| Area | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Presentation architecture | The reusable contracts are implemented in the geographic core and Apple adapter. Consumers supply grouping, political values, styles and surrounding UI. |
| National editorial labels | At 370 × 380 across 60 elections, full placements grew from 949 to 1,152 and suppressions fell from 528 to 375. All measured host scenes retained containment and collision checks. |
| Exploratory labels | The exploratory resolver matched the archived geographic placement decisions. Adaptive viewport eligibility and native selection remain available. |
| State maps | Alabama 2004 and 2024, Texas, Florida, Michigan, Virginia, Connecticut, Alaska and Hawaii use their projected feature bounds. The 2004 vintage fallback stays disclosed. |
| Local inspection | Autauga County, Alaska State House District 1 and TX-18 exercise geographic inspection and stable identity. |
| Context | Separate 2024 Census mainland outlines stay outside primary picking and rendering buffers. The island and Alaska inset policies retain isolation. |
| Geographic correctness | All 14 regional mesh payloads, 14 PROJ references, 70,000 selection probes, 690,408 containment probes and 60 presidential snapshots passed. |
| Rendering and performance | Dense simulator callback cadence stayed near 59 per second at 1× and 8×. Editorial layout reached 0.40 ms P95. Context preparation took 0.19 to 0.42 seconds in the complete capture cohort, with a later 7.77-second outlier in an incomplete capture. |
| Responsive presentation | Both phone sizes passed portrait review and the landscape layout test. iPad portrait and two actual macOS window sizes were reviewed. iPad landscape remains open because the simulator's windowed configuration retained a portrait app viewport after rotation. |
| Visual acceptance | Private matched comparisons and the original reference images are available for owner review. Editorial acceptance and the final small-state and split-allocation art remain open. |
| Device production gate | The signed route awaits an available physical iPhone or iPad. The simulator and unsigned Release builds cover the research workflow. |

Historical election reconciliation, vintage boundaries for older Alaska reporting districts, outlying territory presentation, redistribution rights, Ballotics-Data publication and Android parity remain separate qualification gates. Production accessibility qualification, including VoiceOver and system Reduce Motion on a signed device, remains open. The exact legacy visualization action bindings and Shift formula remain unverified.

For 0.4D, focus owner review on small-state and split-allocation artwork, the readability of the key and a bounded set of the most consequential remaining national omissions. Carry forward the exact geographic contracts and run the signed-device route when hardware is available.
