# Historical county data audit

The input is the preserved Ballotics copy of `US_HistCounties_Gen05.json`. Its size is 39,904,769 bytes and its SHA-256 is `af037e36183c9274d8be905b966ba775b548b6ec5210360c5814a7b952133a7b`. Its records and endpoint match the Newberry Atlas of Historical County Boundaries national compilation. The GeoJSON export history and publication rights are separate provenance questions. [Newberry national metadata](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/US_HistCounties_Metadata1.htm).

## Date semantics and coverage

Both endpoints are **inclusive civil days**. Newberry defines the fields as the first and last day of a version. The preserved export encodes them as `YYYY-MM-DDT00:00:00.000Z`. The integer fields `START_N` and `END_N` agree with all stored date strings. The lab compares Gregorian day numbers and carries the Atlas's reported effective dates through the pipeline. [Newberry field definitions](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/OH_Metadata2.htm).

- Earliest start is **1629-03-04**. The latest start is **2000-04-01**. The last covered day is **2000-12-31**, with 3,141 records active that day. All 17,727 records have both dates. There are 120 one-day versions and zero reversed ranges. The 3,141 rows ending at the archive cutoff have a finite end in this dataset.
- There are 13,769 adjacent successive versions whose new start follows the older record's inclusive end by one day. There are 68 longer gaps within an entity and zero same-entity overlaps. Geographic coverage changes over time. For example, Colbert County disappears after November 28, 1867 and returns January 24, 1870, following abolition and re-creation. The lab leaves gaps intact. [Newberry Alabama commentary](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/AL_Commentary.htm).
- Seven January 1 research snapshots from 1788 to 2000 are supported. **2024 is outside coverage.** Custom dates can reach the colonial records. The 1788 and 1800 maps show represented county-level jurisdictions. Full colonial territorial claims and many areas outside organized county coverage are beyond the source's scope. Alaska's version dates follow census conventions described in the atlas. [Newberry national commentary](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/US_Commentary.htm).
- The application accepts an exact date with a supplied citation or an explicit snapshot label. Each preset is a research snapshot. Verified election-date evidence, multi-day election periods, and alignment to the geography of election returns belong to a separate election-data contract. Legislative and cartographic uncertainties in the original Atlas still apply. [Newberry compiler commentary](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/RI_commentary.htm).

## Identity and simultaneous jurisdictions

The archive has **3,890 distinct `ID` values** and **17,727 unique `ID_NUM` records and `(ID, VERSION)` pairs**. 2,868 entities have multiple versions. `ID` links versions for this source revision, `VERSION` orders them within that entity, and `ID_NUM` identifies the selected record. The archive has 15,790 Polygon and 1,937 MultiPolygon features. There are 14,452 distinct exact decoded geometry representations.

- 193 entities change `NAME`, 1,283 change `STATE_TERR`, and two change `FIPS` values across their versions. `FIPS` has 1,715 blank values, and `CROSS_REF` has 16,305 distinct values. FIPS and cross-reference codes are unsuitable as record identity. The inspector surfaces `FULL_NAME`, `NAME`, `ID`, `ID_NUM`, `VERSION`, `STATE_TERR`, `CNTY_TYPE`, dates, `CHANGE` and `CITATION` where present. The archive contains `NAME_START`; it has no `ABBR_NAME` field.
- The exact-geometry audit found 48 pairs with identical coordinates and a shared active day. For example, records 6860 and 12250 coincide in Orleans Territory at the 1808 snapshot. These Concordia records belong to overlapping parish and county systems. The Atlas also records concurrent county, parish, and district jurisdiction in South Carolina. Different entities can also partially overlap in disputes. Date filtering preserves active records from each jurisdiction. The lab confirms rendered candidates against the original coordinate rings, including holes, then uses render order if several candidates still contain the tap. Exhaustive spatial intersection analysis and an election-specific rule for choosing a jurisdiction or surfacing multiple hits remain open. [Newberry national commentary](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/US_Commentary.htm), [Louisiana chronology](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/LA_Consolidated_Chronology.htm), [Iowa boundary-dispute commentary](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/IA_Commentary.htm).
- The standalone engine checks required identity and range fields, numeric-date agreement when provided, uniqueness of version identifiers, feature geometry and geographic coordinate ranges. Synthetic open ends must be explicit JSON null with an explicit archive coverage end. Missing dates, contradictory ranges, malformed collections, and duplicate record or entity/version keys fail validation. Same-date geometries belonging to distinct source records are retained.
