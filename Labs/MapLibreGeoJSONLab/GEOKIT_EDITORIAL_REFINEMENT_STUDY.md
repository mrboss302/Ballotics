# Electoral editorial refinement

The eight-point internal type floor is retained. Native measurement and attributed drawing share the medium-weight tabular-number font and calibrated line height. Solid fills select dark or light text by relative luminance. Striped fill uses a lighter, warm neutral plate. Internal artwork and ordinary labels share the same collision constraints. The annotation row prioritizes jurisdictions where a qualified count was suppressed, then outstanding allocation artwork, then counts represented by a compact abbreviation. The Results panel preserves the rest.

The broad 0.4C headline counts combine geometry and content actions. The visible qualified-value metric counts a reported value once even if available in multiple places. A displayed assigned count with unresolved recipient is tracked separately. The study reports baseline omissions separately from the candidate's narrower map viewport after reserving real 44-point controls.

No authored geographic overrides were introduced. The conservative leader policy, dedicated key and results inspector provide fallback for narrow inland geography. An authored exception should follow owner review of recurring problems and should attach to a geographic revision or validity interval.
