## What changed

The lab now uses the package's validated geometry, regional projection and composition, spatial index, camera values and label resolver. Thematic National retains the approved regional scales and the separate Equal-Scale Research option. Focus filters both drawing and picking to the selected region while preserving the existing mesh, projected coordinates and feature IDs. Returning to National restores all three regions. The macOS DistrictAtlas consumer loaded 441 Census records, displayed 436 mainland/Alaska/Hawaii districts and framed TX-18 by stable GEOID. Its fills are explicitly synthetic. [National district view](evidence/geokit-0.4a/district-national.png) · [TX-18 detail](evidence/geokit-0.4a/district-tx18.png)

Use a bounded pole search with alternative interior candidates, full measured-rectangle checks and revision caching. At 370 × 380 the pool admitted 949 full geographic fits, versus 760 for the single pole and 368 for the legacy vertex mean. Keep structural editorial decisions such as the ordered small-state key. Use a revision-scoped geographic rule only where an owner-reviewed placement needs correction. The nine external tile roles do not count as nine manual anchor fixes.

For dense counties the three-trial median thematic Metal callback rate reached 59.0/s at 1× and 58.9/s at 8×, compared with 56.5 and 56.6/s at baseline. Dense request-to-GPU-completion time increased from 967.7 to 990.3 ms. Core Graphics national district navigation was slower in its single comparative trial. The native 2024 label layout used 0.3 ms at the median and 0.5 ms at P95. One of 1,017 updates exceeded 4 ms, reaching 4.8 ms. A 120-second stress run completed 100 year changes and 50 source changes with zero mismatches or timeouts. Interpret the raw per-renderer timings alongside the host and cohort limitations. ## Next bounded milestone

For 0.4B, move the existing Apple mesh preparation and renderer ownership behind a narrow adapter. Keep the pinned tessellator, shader inputs and Core Graphics fallback as controls. Gate the move on the same 14 geometry inputs and measured year/profile/selection reuse. Take the national dense, 8× navigation and label routes to a signed physical device. Use the owner's review to select a small set of high-value historical or compact-viewport adjustments before expanding label placement.

Core Graphics path membership remains an Apple dependency. Territorial projection and framing for American Samoa, Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands remain open. Historical election reconciliation, historic Alaska legislative district boundaries, source redistribution rights, Ballotics-Data publication and Android implementation/parity remain separate requirements. The 0.3Q branch retains its complete implementation and evidence.
