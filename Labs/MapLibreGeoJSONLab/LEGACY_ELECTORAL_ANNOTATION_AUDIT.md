# Legacy Ballotics electoral annotations

The preserved iOS repository was verified at commit 4d9f44c355b9b0047d23eb7fa33c82d5a9747c3d. The iCloud 2.4 snapshot includes corresponding key, split configuration, electoral cache and presidential renderer source. Its local Xcode settings identify version 2.4. The recovered 3.0 copy contains interactive and contingent-election helpers and an Elections call site for the presidential renderer. The original 3.0 presidential renderer is still unverified. [Preserved 2.3.1 source](https://github.com/mrboss302/balloticsIOS/tree/4d9f44c355b9b0047d23eb7fa33c82d5a9747c3d)

| Recovered component | Verified design or behavior | 0.4D treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.x StateMapKey and StateMapKeyLarge | Horizontal and vertical keys use VT, NH, MA, RI, CT, NJ, DE, MD, DC in that order. The ordinary 22-point rectangle centers the count and places the postal abbreviation underneath. A second rectangle was used for a legacy split entry. | Square artwork with a separate 44-point interaction rectangle. Responsive wrapping follows available dimensions. |
| 2.x SmallStates and InteractiveMap | Stable state IDs connected the external button, interactive colors and totals. The interactive views maintain several independent color and vote-update branches. | One native control selects the existing geographic feature. The annotation model shares election state. |
| 2.x SplitVoteKeyShape, SplitVoteKeyView, and PresElectionResultsMap | Maine uses two statewide slots plus two district slots. Nebraska uses two statewide slots plus three district slots, with a specialized arrangement. The historical renderer draws numerous jurisdiction- and year-specific split graphics. | Square units derive from allocation records. Measured placement replaces the historical offsets. |
| 2.x LabelAdjustment and ElectoralVoteCache | Configurations mix device idiom, screen-point offsets, colors, years and exceptions. The cache loads electoral data separately from shapes. | Versioned overrides remain available. The milestone uses measured anchors and preserves consumer ownership of election data. |
| 2.x contingent maps | House state-delegation voting uses separate rules. The historical view distinguishes blank or split delegations and marks the decision threshold. | House decision and ballot-basis details stay in the election layer and Results. Presidential squares retain the presidential basis. |
| Recovered 3.0 interactive label helper | The four Maine and five Nebraska slots are explicit. Stored state and district IDs supply their colors. The helper contains display-size offsets. | Retain the visual unit and stable identity with measured placement and explicit source unit types. |
| Recovered 3.0 contingent helpers and owner screenshot | The helper draws split or blank representation. The screenshot corroborates the nine-state key, square count field, muted composition and compact Maine artwork. | Visual and interaction evidence informs composition. Counts and participation come from the versioned research packages. |

The 2.4 check was read-only. It establishes the presence and behavior of relevant source files. Build provenance and the missing 3.0 presidential renderer remain open.
